Analysis of the expense claims checker; no rewrite decided.

The system: an expense claims completeness check that exists three times, once per language, in
the repositories js, java and go. Each is one small HTTP app: GET / serves a page with a form, POST
/claims takes {employee, lines: [{amount, category, receipt?}]} and answers {complete}. A claim is
complete when every line is: a per-diem line needs no receipt, a line under the receipt threshold
needs none, any other line needs one. A receipt attaches only under the claim's own URL and by the
claim's own employee; a refused attachment is logged. The three implementations are meant to be the
same business; where they differ, that is a finding. Record each repository's facts on their own:
a branch in java or go is a guard of its own even when it reads as the js one.

Why: understand and model the business before any decision to merge the three into one, or to
rebuild. No target stack chosen.

How the source runs: it does not run in this analysis. The directory of checkouts is read only;
no suite level, no records.

Default Disposition: keep. The interface: not decided.
